# Recurring payments

The recurring-rule model, the engine that generates occurrences, and creating/cancelling rules.

:::voice[From the author]
Initially, I thought that the recurring payment was an easy implementation and didn't give it much
thought until the planning stage. Claude gave me the option of using a separate table to store
the **recurring rule** so that past history would not be lost when a rule is deleted. This made me
realise that AI can help brainstorm ideas so that they are ***well thought through***.
:::

# Cleanup & Polish

Fidelity passes against the design handoff and standardizing every popup onto the shared BlurPopup.

:::voice[From the author]
This section is for cleaning up and polishing the UI. As mentioned, one of the challenges that I have faced
was that Claude did not follow the **design handoff** well. It was making its own judgement calls instead of
following the design handoff. Things got better after I included screenshots in the cleanup.

However, I do not think that the screenshot itself was a good enough tactic to ensure adherence to the
handoff. Including a statement to *always validate with the handoff* in the `CLAUDE.md` file
would be a better practice since it is the file that it will refer to before editing the code.
:::

# Home & Entry

The transaction list (Home) and the full-screen add-expense page with its custom numeric keypad.

:::voice[From the author]
I really liked the simplicity of `Dime` for the Home and Entry pages, which is why I followed its principle
of simplicity. However, I wanted a slight twist to make it look like a cash register. This was
implemented in two separate PRs.

Behind the Build

plop was built as a portfolio piece for a disciplined, AI-assisted workflow: every feature went through a written spec and plan before any code, one branch and PR per slice, with tests and lint gating main.

The workflow​

From the author

I started with writing down the User Stories, followed by the different functionalities. I decided to work on Swift to build an iOS app instead of React Native because of my previous course that made me more familiar with Swift.

After deciding on the Tech Stack, I ideated what I wanted to see on my UI. I decided to give Claude Design a try. I sent my requirements to Claude to generate a good prompt and the results were unexpectedly well done!

I installed the superpowers skill and started the workflow of design → specs → implementation. I built a roadmap at the beginning so that I do not lose sight of the goal and milestones.

What worked​

From the author

Specs planning allowed Claude to not lose context about what to do and everything was very hands off after reading the specs. Claude also provided a brief summary of what it had planned and confirmed multiple design choices with me before proceeding. This made the process a lot more thought through.

What was hard​

From the author

There were mainly two things that I found hard.

The first was that it was not really able to follow UI descriptions well at first even though I have included the Claude Design handoff to it. I went to take screenshots and attached to the handoff which helped the accuracy of implementation.

The second thing that I found hard was that it felt very easy to lose the context of the application for me, the human. The implementation was really fast and that reduced the time that I have spent with the code and thinking about it. This meant that I was unfamiliar with the implementation. Since this was supposed to be an experimental attempt, I allowed AI to implement all of the code without changing a single line myself.

What I'd change​

From the author

I would try to include the roadmap into the CLAUDE.md file so that Claude updates it along the way and for me to take reference to. I would also maybe let Claude give me a non-technical and technical summary of what has been implemented, what a file was doing. I would also attach screenshots early on into the development so that I do not have to modify the UI again after the initial implementation.

Advice to someone trying this​

From the author

Just do it! It is perfectly fine to give AI coding a try, as it might just be an essential skill that employers look out for right now! Build something on your own with AI now that it can greatly reduce your development time.
